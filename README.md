# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

A simple interest calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

* **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money.
* **Rate (R):** The annual interest rate in percentage.
* **Time (T):** The time period in years.

## Example

* Principal: 1000
* Rate: 5%
* Time: 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

**Result:** The simple interest is 100.

## Purpose

This project demonstrates the basic calculation of simple interest using a mathematical formula.
